\cvsection{About me}

\begin{cvparagraph}
I am a PhD student in Physics at Chongqing University and expect to graduate in June 2028. My research focuses on gravitational-wave signals from moving compact binaries in hierarchical and astrophysical environments, particularly the effects of source motion, external gravitational fields, and environmental interactions on long-duration signals observed by space-based detectors. My work spans source dynamics, waveform calculations, detector response, and parameter estimation, with additional interests in tests of general relativity.
\end{cvparagraph}
