# About me

<!-- <img src="https://wujie3375.github.io/caihanlin.jpg" class="floatpic" width="360" height="480"> -->
 

Hi there! Welcome to my personal corner of the internet!

I’m **Jie Wu**, a PhD student in Physics at **Chongqing University**, expecting to graduate in June 2028. My research focuses on GW modeling, data analysis, and parameter estimation, particularly source motion, environmental effects, space-based detectors, and tests of gravity. In simple terms, I work on ways to read some of the faintest signals in the universe—and understand the stories behind them.

Most days, this means working with signals, simulations, and code. On good days, it feels a bit like decoding messages from the universe. On other days, it feels like debugging at 2 a.m. with coffee as a co-author.

Outside research, the same curiosity draws me to the sky, engineering, photography, and good stories.

- {% include research_interest_icon.html name="modeling" %} **GW modeling**
